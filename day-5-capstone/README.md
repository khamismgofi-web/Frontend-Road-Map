# Day 5 — Capstone: Combine Days 1–4

**Goal:** Prove the first four days add up to something real, not four disconnected exercises. Combine your dashboard, navbar, auth cards, and API-connected component into one coherent mini-app.

## Requirements

- The Navbar (Day 2) sits at the top of the app
- A route or toggle shows the Login/Signup cards (Day 3)
- After "logging in" (this can be simulated — no real auth backend required), the user sees the Dashboard (Day 1)
- The Dashboard includes your API-connected component from Day 4, with its loading/error/loaded states intact
- Consistent styling across all four pieces — this is the part that's actually being tested: do your earlier components look and feel like one product, or four separate exercises glued together?
- Use Tailwind utility classes throughout, consistent with the earlier days

## Not Required

- Real backend authentication
- Persisting login state across a page refresh (in-memory state for the session is enough)

## Submit

1. Put your project in `submissions/<your-name>/day-5/`
2. Open a PR titled `Day 5 — Capstone — <your name>`
3. In the PR description, briefly note anything you changed from your original Day 1–4 versions to make them fit together, and why

## Deadline

By end of day, Day 5. This is the one the Frontend Lead will look at most closely — it's the clearest signal of whether you can ship something that holds together, not just isolated pieces.
